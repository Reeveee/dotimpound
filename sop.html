Department of Transportation / Tow Truck Division – Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

DEPARTMENTAL POLICYACTIVE SOP

011. Purpose

The purpose of this SOP is to establish uniform procedures, expectations, and professional standards for all members of the Department of Transportation (DOT) and Tow Truck Division within the State of San Andreas.

022. Mission Statement

To maintain public safety and roadway efficiency by providing towing services, roadside assistance, traffic control, and public service operations with professionalism, respect, and integrity.

033. Chain of Command

  1. Director of Transportation
  2. Assistant Director of DOT
  3. Crew Chief
  4. DOT Master Operator
  5. DOT Senior Operator
  6. DOT Operator
  7. Road Side Assistant Operator

All members are required to follow the chain of command when reporting incidents, requesting assistance, addressing maintenance issues, or handling disciplinary matters.

055. Vehicle Guidelines

  • All DOT vehicles must display proper department markings and emergency lighting.
  • Emergency lights may only be used while responding to scenes, clearing hazards, or conducting traffic control.
  • Reckless driving, excessive speeding, or misuse of sirens/lights is prohibited.
  • Vehicle inspections must be completed before and after each shift.
  • Any damages or malfunctions must be reported immediately.

066. Dispatch & Response

  • Channel 6 will be the dedicated radio channel for DOT
  • All active calls must be announced over the department radio.
  • Operators are authorized to patrol the entire state for disabled vehicles and roadway hazards.
  • Priority response calls include:
  • Law Enforcement tow requests
  • Blocked roadways
  • Traffic hazards
  • Major accidents
  • Vehicle owners or law enforcement must authorize all tows.
  • Law Enforcement vehicles may never be towed without permission from the ranking officer on scene or authorized supervisor!

077. Scene Operations

  • Park tow vehicles in a safe and visible location.
  • Activate warning lights when working roadside scenes.
  • Establish traffic control using cones, barriers, or vehicle placement.
  • Maintain communication with all units on scene.
  • Safety of officers, civilians, EMS, and operators is the top priority.
  • Take photographs or log evidence when necessary for roleplay documentation.

088. Towing Guidelines

  • Operators must log:
  • Vehicle plate number
  • Vehicle description
  • Location
  • Reason for tow
  • Vehicles must be delivered to the Impound Lot or designated storage area.
  • Impound reports must include:
  • Vehicle ID
  • Date/Time
  • Reason for impound
  • Operator name

099. Roadside Assistance

DOT may respond to the following service calls:

  • Vehicle breakdowns (engine failure, flat tire, no fuel, dead battery)
  • Road debris or roadway hazards
  • Traffic signal/sign malfunctions
  • Road closures
  • Accident recovery assistance

All operators must act professionally, respectfully, and verify caller information before providing service.

1010. Conduct

  • Maintain professionalism at all times.
  • Respect civilians, law enforcement, and emergency service personnel.
  • No cop-baiting, trolling, random towing, or unrealistic roleplay scenarios.
  • Remain in-character while on duty.
  • Unauthorized towing, abuse of powers, or griefing will not be tolerated.
  • Represent the department in a positive manner at all times.

1111. Reports and Logs

At the end of each shift or assigned call, operators must submit required logs including:

  • Operator name
  • Date/Time
  • Call type
  • Location
  • Action taken

If property damage, escalation, arrests, or complaints occur, a supervisor must be notified immediately.

1212. Disciplinary Action

Violations of this SOP may result in:

  • Verbal Warning
  • Written Warning
  • Strike
  • Termination from DOT / Tow Division

Punishment severity will depend on the violation and prior disciplinary history.

1313. Conclusion

Every DOT and Tow Division member represents the safety, professionalism, and efficiency of the State of San Andreas. By following this SOP, all members help ensure fair, realistic, and organized operations for the community.
