SA DOT v4.35.31

ROADSIDe ASSISTANCE GAME
- New Roadside Assist dashboard section.
- Pulls eligible active disabled/mechanical roadside tow requests into its own dispatch queue.
- Operators can claim, go en route, arrive, then launch an interactive roadside procedure.
- Walk-up assist mode works without a dispatch request.
- Interactive procedures: Battery/Jump Start, Tyre Pressure, Fuse/Electrical, Winch Recovery, Fuel Delivery.
- Completion outcome and recent service history stored per operator browser.
- Linked dispatch calls can be closed after a successful assist or left open when a tow is still required.

CAD AUDIO
- Browser-generated tones, no audio files required.
- New tow, road closure, roadside, vehicle payment, and emergency tones.
- Global CAD Audio mute switch in the top bar; preference saved in browser.
- Visual CAD notification cards remain visible when audio is muted.
- Dispatch poll checks tow requests, road closures, and payments every 5 seconds.

No new Supabase table is required for this version.
